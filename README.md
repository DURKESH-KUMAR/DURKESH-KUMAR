## `01 / THE DEVELOPER BEHIND THE SCREEN`

I'm **Durkesh Kumar S**, a Game Developer who enjoys bringing ideas to life through code, creativity, and technology.

For me, game development is more than making something look good on screen. It's about understanding what happens behind the scenes — how gameplay mechanics interact, how systems communicate, how environments come alive, and how every component works together to create a seamless experience.

My development journey revolves around two important worlds:

**THE TECHNICAL SIDE**

Gameplay programming, application architecture, interactive systems, debugging, performance optimization, and problem-solving.

**THE CREATIVE SIDE**

3D modeling, environment development, visual design, interactive experiences, and bringing digital worlds to life.

I enjoy working at the intersection of both.

## `03 / BEHIND THE GAMEPLAY`

I am particularly interested in the engineering that makes interactive applications reliable and responsive.

Some of the technical areas I work with and explore include:

- **Gameplay Logic:** Designing and implementing interactive behavior.
- **System Architecture:** Organizing scripts and components for maintainability.
- **Scene Management:** Managing scene transitions and application states.
- **Memory Management:** Understanding allocations, object lifecycles, and memory profiling.
- **Performance Optimization:** Investigating bottlenecks and improving runtime behavior.
- **Communication Systems:** Working with data exchange and device connectivity.
- **Debugging:** Identifying unexpected behavior and improving application stability.

I believe a good game is not just about what the player sees. It's also about the engineering that makes everything work together.
